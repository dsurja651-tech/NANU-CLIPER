Is NanuCut Clipper completely free? ▼
Yes! NanuCut Clipper is 100% open-source and free forever. You run it on your own computer or server with your own free API keys, saving you over $350/year compared to paid alternatives.
How does the Opus-style facecam split work? ▼
We use OpenCV's YuNet neural network to detect the streamer's face across the video. The top 1080x960 panel centers on the facecam with zero gameplay bleed, while the bottom panel shows pure gameplay.
Can I export clips in 4K UHD? ▼
Yes! NanuCut Clipper supports 720p, 1080p, 2K (1440p), and 4K (2160p UHD) 60fps vertical exports so your Shorts and Reels look ultra-crisp.
